About Me

I go by Sharsky, a self-taught rap artist and music producer crafting everything out of my own home studio. No outside engineers, no ghostwriters, no extra hands. If you hear it, I made it—the beats, the basslines, the vocals, the lyrics, the mix and master, the full arrangement.

By eliminating the middleman, I ensure that every track is an unfiltered reflection of my vision. From the first kick drum to the final vocal layer, the process is entirely hands-on. This independent workflow allows me to experiment with sound design and storytelling in a way that traditional studio environments don't always permit.

Operating as a one-man army means mastering the technical side of the craft just as much as the creative side. Whether it's fine-tuning the EQ on a vocal chain or engineering a punchy low-end for a new beat, the goal is always professional-grade quality delivered from a personal space.
